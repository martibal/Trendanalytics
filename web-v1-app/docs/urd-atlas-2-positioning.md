# Urd Atlas 2.0 positioning

## What Urd Atlas is

A deterministic daily blockchain network-state layer.

It can be used in three ways:

- Explore: read chain conditions in the browser.
- Analyze: download and use calendars, summaries, charts and notebooks.
- Integrate: join point-in-time features into models, backtests and production monitoring.

## What Urd Atlas is not

- Not raw on-chain data.
- Not an intraday trading signal.
- Not a buy/sell recommendation.
- Not price prediction.
- Not a replacement for a quant or analyst.

## Core sentence

Urd Atlas turns blockchain activity into a daily network-state layer that can be read in the browser, downloaded into analyst workflows, or integrated into models and production systems.

## Technical sentence

Join Urd Atlas on date and chain, filter by confidence and freshness, then measure how models, reports, dashboards or protocol metrics behave across STABLE, HEATING, CONGESTED, CHEAP and UNKNOWN conditions.

## No-pipeline sentence

Use Urd Atlas to understand, summarize and report blockchain network conditions without building a data pipeline first.

## Production sentence

Use Urd Atlas as a reproducible point-in-time feature layer for regime-conditioned model evaluation, monitoring, research segmentation and feature-store ingestion.
